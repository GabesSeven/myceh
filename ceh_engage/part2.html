Part 2 of CEH Engage covers System Hacking, Malware Threats, Sniffing, Social Engineering, and Denial-of-Service modules.

In this part, you must exploit vulnerabilities identified in the last part and use various network/system/human exploitation techniques to gain access to the target's systems.

CEH Engage - Part IV

Part 4 of CEH Engage covers Hacking Wireless Networks, Hacking Mobile Platforms, IoT and OT Hacking, Cloud Computing, and Cryptography modules.

In this part, you must analyze wireless packet captures, use different attack vectors to exploit mobile devices, and audit IoT and OT systems/networks for known threats.

You need to note all the information discovered in this part of the CEH Engage.

Note: Attempt this part after completing all 20 modules of the CEH program.
